ABOUT CO-GIG

Building Stronger Local Communities

Co-Gig is a cooperative gig service platform designed to connect households, farmers and communities with trusted local workers.

Our platform creates opportunities for local people to earn income by offering their skills while helping customers find reliable services close to them.
